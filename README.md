# Game design document 
 
## General idea (the game core): 
This game is about a late-night technician trying to survive a studio filled with anomalies and creepy, shifting puppets, while recovering the old tapes of unaired corrupted episodes of the puppet show, almost 50 years after the show was abruptly cancelled and all the cast and production team vanished without a trace.
Target audience: Fans of analog horror and psychological horror games

## Game type: 
Category: Hardcore

Medium: Singleplayer

Platform: PC

## Genre: 
Mechanical: 3D 

Dynamic: Survival/Strategy

Aesthetic: Horror/Psychological

## Game Mechanics (players/ characters): 
The player: the protagonist, a technician; tasked with restoring the old tapes

The puppets, the main characters in the show the player is trying to restore

## Starting state: 
The player starts sitting at the desk inside the central Editing Room. The restoration deck monitor shows 0% progress. The first puppet show tape is inserted, and the surveillance monitors are active. 

## Ending state: 
Victory: The tape restoration progress bar hits 100% without the system crashing or the player being killed, unlocking the final broadcast truth and the studio exit. 

Game Over: Failing to address anomalies in time causes the video signal to break completely into permanent static (100% audio-visual distortion), or triggers a fatal jumpscare if the paranoia level peaks.  

Goals: Successfully restore the vintage puppet show cassettes to 100% while surviving, thus finding out the truth about the show’s abrupt end

Player Control: Monitor clicks and certain keyboard keys to interact with the tapes in viewing mode or with the objects in the studio, WASD 3D movement while not at the editor desk

A to B: cyclical: sit at the desk; watch the tape until anomaly happens; go into the office to solve the anomaly while avoiding the puppets; return to the desk

## Challenges: 
Hallucinated sounds, footsteps, creepy/jumpscare scenes in the tapes, and looking through cameras only to realize a static puppet has suddenly shifted angles or moved rooms entirely when you weren't looking. 

Camera perspective: First-Person View

Story: The player is hired to retrieve the lost master tapes of a 1980s puppet show, almost 50 years after its last airing (the office became public property so the local authorities had jurisdiction). The show was yanked off the air permanently when a bizarre broadcast hijacking occurred mid-episode, followed up by the mysterious disappearance of all crew and actors working for it. Working alone in the decaying studio, the technician finds that the old puppets left behind aren't completely unmoving, but reacting to the tape restoration, causing disturbing anomalies to manifest physically across the building. 

Story Type: Linear story

## Storytelling methods
Cutscenes and cinematics: parts of the episodes, with their anomalies

In-game events: props shifting places, and background TVs turning on with creepy messages or cries for help

Dialogue/Text: distorted tape audio, reversed melodies, and eerie audio static playing through the technician’s headphones.
